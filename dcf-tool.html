Methodology & DCF Mechanics

This tool uses a standard Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF) framework. Over the 5-year explicit forecast horizon, revenues are projected based on the compound growth assumption, EBIT margins determine operating profitability, and Net Operating Profit After Tax (NOPAT) is adjusted for reinvestment into capital expenditures and gold working capital.

The terminal value utilizes the Gordon Growth formula, discounted back to present value using WACC. Deducting net debt (which includes bank gold-on-lease liabilities) yields equity value, which divided by diluted shares yields intrinsic value per share.
