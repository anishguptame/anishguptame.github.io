Why Multiple Divergence Exists in Indian Retail

High optical P/E multiples (such as Trent at ~148x vs Kalyan at ~68x) are driven by market expectations of runway length and reinvestment economics. Trent commands a premium due to Zudio's exceptional unit economics and ~40%+ revenue compounding, whereas regional jewellers trade at a discount due to higher working-capital debt requirements.

Titan vs. Direct Jewellery Peers

Titan (Tanishq) commands a premium valuation over Kalyan Jewellers because of its franchised store model, superior customer trust moat, and gold-on-lease financing structure that drives an industry-leading 28.4% ROCE without speculative gold inventory exposure.
