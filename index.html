01 — About

Learning in public, one model at a time.

I'm Anish, an undergraduate studying Economics and Finance at Christ University, Bangalore. I want to work in equity research and investment banking, where I'd be figuring out what businesses are actually worth and why markets sometimes price them differently.

Rather than just reading about valuation, I'm building it. Every project on this site comes from something I've studied, with the assumptions, the reasoning and the mistakes written out, so anyone can follow how I got from the financial statements to a price target.
