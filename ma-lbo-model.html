Investment Banking Modeling Principles

Accretion / Dilution Mechanics: A transaction is EPS accretive if the pro-forma earnings per share exceeds the standalone acquirer EPS. In an all-stock deal, a transaction is accretive whenever the acquirer's P/E multiple is higher than the effective acquisition P/E of the target. When funded via cash/debt, the hurdle is whether the after-tax cost of debt is lower than the target's earnings yield.

LBO Return Value Drivers: Private Equity buyouts generate returns across three distinct levers: (1) Deleveraging (using company free cash flows to pay down acquisition debt, transferring enterprise value to equity value), (2) Operational Growth (EBITDA expansion), and (3) Multiple Expansion (exiting at an EV/EBITDA multiple higher than entry).
